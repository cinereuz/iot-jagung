    Stage 1 of 5

    Image Acquisition & Smart ROI Square Standardization

    Standardizes arbitrary camera resolutions into a centered 224×224 square frame while preserving background borders for accurate Otsu polarity estimation.

    1A. Raw Input Image -

    Original uploaded frame before aspect-ratio standardization.

    1B. Standardized ROI Frame 224 × 224 px

    Smart centered square crop resized via area/cubic interpolation to 224×224 px.

    Why Smart ROI Standardization? Directly stretching non-square photos distorts kernel geometry (aspect ratio & solidity), while tight cropping removes the background border needed to detect whether the surface is dark cloth or white paper.
    Stage 2 of 5

    Image Preprocessing: Color Conversion & Gaussian Noise Filtering

    Converts the standardized BGR image to luminance grayscale and applies Gaussian smoothing to suppress high-frequency camera sensor and cloth-weave noise.

    2A. Standardized RGB 3 Channels (BGR)

    Input 224×224 color image.

    2B. Grayscale Conversion Single Channel (0–255)

    Luminance conversion: Y = 0.299R + 0.587G + 0.114B.

    2C. Gaussian Blur Filtered Kernel 5×5, σ=0

    Smoothed grayscale image ready for Otsu thresholding.

    Pixel Intensity Histogram (Grayscale vs Gaussian Blur)

    Distribution of pixel intensities across 32 bins (0–255). Smoothing attenuates local intensity spikes.

    Preprocessing Diagnostics & Parameters

    Role of Gaussian Blur: A 5×5 Gaussian low-pass filter prevents fine fabric textures or specular dust from creating false binary blobs during Otsu thresholding, while a 3×3 smoothing on 128×128 crops stabilizes GLCM texture extraction.
    Stage 3 of 5

    Two-Level Segmentation: Otsu + Watershed (Kernels) & HSV K-Means (Mold Lesions)

    Separates individual corn kernels from the background using Warmth-Guided Otsu Thresholding and CLAHE Distance-Transform Watershed, then isolates fungal lesions per kernel via K-Means clustering.

    Sub-Stage 3A: Global Kernel Segmentation (Otsu Thresholding & Watershed Splitting)

    Binary Otsu Foreground Mask Otsu + Morph Open

    Foreground mask after cool-glare suppression, Otsu thresholding, 3×3 elliptical opening, and hole filling.

    Watershed Instance Contours & ROIs 0 Objects

    Individual kernel contours separated via CLAHE crease detection and distance-transform watershed.

    Novelty Detection Gate & Support Vector Machine (RBF-SVM) Classification
